## What this is

A public, dated archive of forensic research on Indian listed companies. Each
file is built from primary documents: annual reports, earnings call
transcripts and exchange filings on BSE and NSE.

## How we work

We read the filings, check the arithmetic across quarters, and note where the
numbers or the narrative don't line up. We keep what a filing says separate
from what we think it means, and we link every figure to the document it came
from.

We do not give buy, sell or hold calls, price targets or valuations of what a
share should be worth.

## Proof of dates

Every file on this site is stored in a public GitHub repository. Its history
shows when each file was added and every change made since. That history,
not the date printed on the page, is the record.

## Holdings

Each company page states whether the author holds shares in that company.

## Disclaimer

This site publishes factual analysis of public filings. It is not investment
advice and does not recommend buying, selling or holding any security. The
author is not registered with SEBI as a Research Analyst or Investment
Adviser. Figures come from company filings and may contain errors; check the
linked source before relying on any of them.

## Using this material with AI tools

Every page has a plain-text version, and the whole archive is listed in
[llms.txt](/llms.txt). You're welcome to point AI tools at it. Please cite the
page and its date.
